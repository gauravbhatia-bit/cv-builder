# Base Cover Letter

The reference letter. Every custom letter starts from this one and changes only the three marked
parts. **Do not change the structure, the voice or the football sentence.** Voice rules are in
`STYLE.md`: no em dashes, no banned phrases, under 300 words.

Fill per job (the first three markers are replaced; the football line is edited, never removed):

| Marker | What goes there |
|---|---|
| `[WHY]` | One honest sentence on why this company and this role, using only what Gaurav has told us. If it cannot be written truthfully, do not send the application. |
| `[PROOF]` | The one achievement that matches the JD best, with one number. Pick from `COVER-LETTER-BUILDER.md` via `RULES.md` section 10. |
| The football sentence | **Already in the letter and never deleted.** Keep it, or swap the wording for the version in the S8 table that fits the JD. Must stay linked to the story before it. |

Swap paragraph 3 for a different story when the JD calls for it. Keep paragraphs 1, 2 and 5.

---

Dear [Hiring Team / Ms or Mr Surname],

[WHY] That is why the [Role] position caught my attention.

I came to data through running a business. For eight years I ran Bhatia Traders, handling purchasing, pricing and stock. I taught myself SQL out of interest, and the first project I gave it was my own sales data, which showed I could have cut stockouts by around 15%. That led me to an MSc in Data Science at GISMA in Potsdam, which I finish in December 2026.

[PROOF] At KnowGuard AI I ran a paired A/B test that came back positive but below the practical-effect threshold I had set before running it, so I reported it as not worth shipping. I would rather give a team an honest answer than a flattering one.

At Troika International I was handed a 500-unit export order to Lithuania that was close to collapsing on delivery time. I moved it to air freight and renegotiated with the client, and it only worked because logistics, the vendor and the client all pulled the same way. That felt familiar from my football years in the third tier with Lonestar FC in Jammu and Kashmir: the result belongs to the whole side.

I am based in Berlin and available full-time from December 2026. I would be glad to talk about how I can help [Company].

Kind regards,
Gaurav Bhatia
