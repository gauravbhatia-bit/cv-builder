Dear Hiring Team,

For the past year I have been trying to explain why German day-ahead power prices moved the way they did after the 2026 Hormuz disruption. Your analysts answer that kind of question every day, with real money behind it, which is why the Energy Market Analyst role stood out.

That question is my MSc thesis at GISMA. I combined an event study, Granger causality and Chronos and XGBoost forecast models in a reproducible Python pipeline to test how the shock reached prices, and I benchmarked it against 2022. Six other energy projects draw on German market and weather data, so this is a domain I keep choosing, not one I picked for this application.

What I care about most is what happens after the model. At KnowGuard AI, a paired A/B test came back positive but below the practical-effect threshold I had set before running it, so I reported it as not worth shipping. I would rather tell a trading desk that a signal is real but too small to act on than hand it a flattering number.

I also know what it takes to work through other people. At Troika International I was handed a 500-unit export order to Lithuania that was close to collapsing on delivery time. I moved it to air freight and renegotiated with the client, and it only worked because logistics, the vendor and the client all pulled the same way. That felt familiar from my football years in the third tier with Lonestar FC in Jammu and Kashmir: the result belongs to the whole side.

I am based in Berlin and available full-time from December 2026. I would be glad to walk your team through the thesis.

Kind regards,
Gaurav Bhatia
